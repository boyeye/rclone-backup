# Privacy Policy

rclone-backup is used solely by its owner for personal backups.

The application accesses Google Drive to list and compare files
and upload personal photos to the owner's backup folder.

Files are transferred from the owner's home server to Google Drive.
OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's devices.
The application does not sell data or share it with other users.

The owner can revoke access in Google Account settings at any time
and delete uploaded backups directly in Google Drive.

Contact: Use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
